Refund & Cancellation Policy

At ZipInAll, we strive to provide a seamless experience for managing your newspaper subscriptions. Please read our policy carefully:

Cancellation

  • You may cancel your subscription at any time through the ZipInAll app.
  • Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period.
  • No partial-month refunds are provided for mid-cycle cancellations.

Refunds

  • If you are not satisfied with our service, you may request a refund within 7 days of payment.
  • Refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.
  • Refunds are not available for partial usage of a subscription month or after 7 days.
  • Refunds will not be issued for amounts less than ₹50 due to transaction costs.

How to Request

Contact us at reach.us.zipinall@gmail.com with your order/subscription details.

We reserve the right to refuse refunds in cases of misuse or violation of our terms.
